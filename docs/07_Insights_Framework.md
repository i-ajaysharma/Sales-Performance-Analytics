## Interview Insight Framework

Do not simply say "North region has the highest sales." Explain:
1. What happened?
2. Where did it happen?
3. Why might it have happened?
4. What should management investigate or do next?
5. What metric would you monitor after the action?

Use the dashboard to validate every insight before presenting it as a business conclusion.
