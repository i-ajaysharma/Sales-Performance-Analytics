
# Business Case

## Company
Fictional omnichannel technology and office-solutions retailer operating across 8 Indian cities.

## Problem
Management currently receives fragmented sales reports and cannot quickly answer:
- Are sales growing?
- Which products/categories drive profit?
- Which regions and channels are underperforming?
- Which salespeople are meeting targets?
- Which customers are valuable or at risk?
- Where are returns/cancellations concentrated?

## Objective
Build an executive-ready analytics solution that converts transaction data into actionable sales, profitability, customer and performance insights.

## Success Criteria
1. One source of truth for sales KPIs.
2. Monthly and YoY trend visibility.
3. Product/category profitability analysis.
4. Salesperson target achievement.
5. Customer segmentation/RFM-style analysis.
6. Regional and channel performance.
7. Interactive drill-down from executive KPI to transaction-level detail.

## Recommended business questions
- What is the revenue and profit trend by month?
- Which categories have high revenue but weak margin?
- Which regions generate the most profit?
- Which salespeople consistently miss target?
- What percentage of orders are returned?
- Which customer tiers contribute most revenue?
- Which channels have the strongest margin?
